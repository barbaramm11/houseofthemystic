Tarot or Birth Chart Reading: Which Fits Your Question?

A grounded starting point for your curiosity.

Both tarot and astrology can create space for reflection, but they begin in different places. Tarot uses cards to explore a current question or pattern together. A birth chart reading starts with your birth date, place, and ideally time to discuss symbolic themes in the chart.

Choose tarot for a present question

If you want to talk through a crossroads, relationship dynamic, or decision you are facing now, a 45-minute intuitive tarot reading may be a useful starting point. You can submit up to two questions ahead of time or choose a general reading. It does not promise a certain future.

Choose a birth chart for a broader theme

The Personality & Patterns reading explores strengths, motivations, and repeating patterns. Career & Direction focuses on interests and ways of working, while Year Ahead uses a solar return chart for birthday-to-birthday reflection. Each is a 75-minute conversation with a brief takeaway PDF.

What if I am unsure?

Think about the scale of your question. If it is specific and immediate, start with tarot. If you want a wider portrait or a year-long lens, explore astrology. You can also ask Barbara which session fits before booking.
