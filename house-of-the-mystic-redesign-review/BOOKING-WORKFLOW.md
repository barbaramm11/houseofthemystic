# Booking and follow-up workflow

## One transaction per service
Preferred eventual flow: a service-specific Calendly event collects the matching Stripe payment while the client chooses a time. The existing Stripe checkout links are a temporary flow for already active services. Do not enable paid Calendly events and keep the separate Stripe checkout buttons for the same offer at the same time.

## Event types and matching intake
- Tarot: 45 minutes, $65; /intake/tarot/
- Personality & Patterns: 75 minutes, $145; /intake/astrology/
- Career & Direction: 75 minutes, $155; /intake/astrology/
- Year Ahead: 75 minutes, $155; /intake/astrology/
- Mediumship: 45 minutes, $95; /intake/mediumship/
- Reiki: 60 minutes, $85; /intake/reiki/
- Animal Reiki: 45 minutes, $65; /intake/animal-reiki/
- Personalized Guided Meditation: 30 minutes, $45; /intake/meditation/

Enable only event types you are ready to fulfill. In each confirmation email, link to https://houseofthemystic.com followed by the matching intake path. Allow enough lead time before astrology sessions for chart preparation and a buffer afterward for notes. Offer the instructions in the client's selected language.

## Preparation text for confirmation emails
- Tarot: Bring a focus or choose a general reading. You may submit up to two questions in the intake. Settle somewhere private where we can talk.
- Astrology: Complete the intake with birth date, exact time if known, birth city/country, and your focus. For Year Ahead, include where you expect to be around your birthday. Submit with enough lead time for preparation.
- Mediumship: If meaningful, speak privately to loved ones beforehand and invite them to join us. This is optional and does not guarantee a connection. Please do not send names, photographs, or stories ahead of time. Find a private, comfortable place to talk.
- Reiki: Share how you feel and your intention in the brief intake. Wear comfortable clothes and settle somewhere you can rest. Keep water nearby if you wish; remove jewelry only if more comfortable. Follow your usual medical guidance. Reiki does not replace medical or mental health care.
- Animal Reiki: Keep your companion in a familiar setting and follow their usual routine. They may move away or rest. Keep water available as usual. Animal Reiki does not replace veterinary care.
- Meditation: Share your intention and any accessibility preference. Choose a quiet place where you can sit or lie comfortably, and do not join while driving.

Test the booking, payment, intake, confirmation, notification, and reschedule path for each service before launch.
