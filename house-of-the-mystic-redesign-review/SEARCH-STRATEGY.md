# Search and content plan — September 2026

## What was actually checked
Google Trends Explore, United States, Web Search, past 12 months, viewed September 28, 2026 (Austin time):
https://trends.google.com/trends/explore?geo=US&q=tarot%20reading,astrology%20reading,reiki,mediumship

Comparing those four exact search terms, normalized average interest was Reiki 57, Tarot reading 39, Astrology reading 4, and Mediumship 3. These are relative Google Trends indices within this comparison, **not monthly search volumes** and not evidence of Austin demand. A low-volume niche term can still be valuable to the business.

Related rising US queries shown with those terms included “what is a tarot reading” (+90%), “what is tarot reading” (+80%), “reiki definition” (+120%), “mediumship definition” (+140%), “what is mediumship” (+60%), and “mediumship near me” (+80%). Trends compares each phrase with its prior period; these percentages are not the amount of total search traffic. Brand names and unrelated queries in the results were excluded.

The new guides answer the relevant explanatory questions. The website states Barbara is Austin-based and sessions are online. It does not imply an in-person location for “near me” searches. Service pages use clear terms such as birth chart reading, solar return, remote Animal Reiki, and evidential mediumship because they match the offerings; no search-volume or growth claim is made for those exact phrases.
