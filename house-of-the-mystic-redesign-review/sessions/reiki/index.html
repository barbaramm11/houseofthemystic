Remote Reiki

Intentional space for relaxation, grounding, and reflection from wherever you feel comfortable.

This remote Reiki session is a quiet hour you can take from home. We begin by talking about your intention and how you feel, allow time for you to rest, then close with a short reflection. I am based in Austin and meet clients online in English or Spanish.

The experience

We begin with a short check-in about your intention and how you are feeling. You then rest during the remote Reiki experience; we close with brief reflections. Reiki is complementary spiritual wellness, not medical care.

What I’ll ask before we meet

Your preferred name, language, intention, any comfort or accessibility needs, and anything you choose to share about how you feel. A diagnosis or medical history is not required.

Preparing for your session

Choose a comfortable place, wear clothes you can relax in, and keep water nearby if you wish. Remove jewelry only if it makes you more comfortable. Follow your usual medical guidance for hydration.

All offerings are for spiritual reflection or complementary wellness. They do not replace medical, veterinary, mental health, legal, financial, or career professionals. Read session policies.

Common questions

Do I need to feel anything during remote Reiki?

No particular sensation or result is required. You can rest in a comfortable place and notice whatever the experience is like for you.

Should I stop my usual care or prepare in a special way?

No. Reiki is complementary spiritual wellness and does not replace medical or mental health care. Wear comfortable clothes; keep water nearby if you wish.
