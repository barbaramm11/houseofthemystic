The experience

Bring a question or choose a general reading. We begin by clarifying the focus, then explore the cards together. You can ask follow-up questions within the appointment. Intuitive impressions may arise; no particular insight is promised.

What I’ll ask before we meet

An optional life area and up to two questions. You can also choose a general reading. Avoid sharing private details about other people.

Preparing for your session

A quiet place where we can talk; a notebook if you like.

Common questions

Do I need to send my question ahead of time?

You may share up to two questions in the intake form after booking, or choose a general reading. We can clarify the focus together when we meet.

Will the reading tell me exactly what will happen?

No. Tarot can offer a new angle on a situation, but it does not guarantee a future event or make a decision for you.
