The experience

We begin with a guardian check-in. Your animal may move, rest, or leave as they choose. I share brief observations afterward. Reiki does not replace veterinary care.

What I’ll ask before we meet

Common questions

Does my pet need to sit still or be on camera?

No. Your animal may sleep, move around, play, or leave. Please do not restrain them for the session.

Can Animal Reiki treat a health problem?

No. It is a complementary spiritual practice and does not diagnose or replace veterinary care. Contact your veterinarian about symptoms or changes in behavior.
