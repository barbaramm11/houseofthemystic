Policies & privacy

Please read the terms for the service you choose before booking.

Session terms

Session prices, durations, and formats are listed on the relevant page. Payment and scheduling must both be complete for a confirmed appointment. No specific spiritual, health, personal, or future outcome is promised.

Rescheduling and cancellation

Requests to reschedule should be made at least 24 hours before the appointment, subject to availability. Client cancellations and missed appointments are nonrefundable under the current policy. If House of the Mystic cancels, you may choose another available time or a refund for the affected session. Please contact Barbara if an exceptional circumstance arises.

Scope of services

Tarot, astrology, and mediumship are for personal and spiritual reflection. Mediumship cannot guarantee contact with a particular person or a specific message. Reiki and guided meditation are complementary wellness practices, not diagnosis or treatment. Animal Reiki does not replace veterinary evaluation or treatment. These services do not replace licensed medical, mental health, veterinary, legal, financial, or career advice. Seek emergency or professional support when needed.

Your privacy

Contact and intake forms may collect your name, email, preferred language, booking details, birth information for astrology, and the limited session information you choose to provide. Netlify may process form submissions and Stripe processes payments. A scheduling provider may process booking details when connected. Do not send medical records or more personal information than needed. Contact Barbara through the contact form for questions about your submitted information. This site currently has no email-marketing signup.

Intake acknowledgment

Intake forms ask you to acknowledge the complementary or reflective scope of your selected service. Checking a box records your acknowledgment; it is not a handwritten signature or medical consent.

Intellectual property

Original text, graphics, meditations, and written resources are for personal use unless written permission says otherwise.
