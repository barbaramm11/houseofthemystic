Before you book

Which session should I choose?

Tarot focuses on a question or current situation. Astrology explores birth-chart themes. Mediumship focuses on a possible connection with someone who has died. Reiki and meditation make room for stillness. Each page describes its scope.

Are sessions available in Spanish?

Yes. Sessions may be held in English or Spanish. Select your preferred language when you book or contact Barbara.

Do you guarantee a particular outcome?

No. Readings and spiritual wellness sessions do not promise a specific message, event, health result, or decision.
