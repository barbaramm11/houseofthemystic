How does remote Animal Reiki work?

Your animal can stay in a familiar environment and take part on their own terms.

I offer remote Animal Reiki from Austin, Texas, so your pet can remain at home. The 45-minute guardian appointment begins with a check-in, leaves room for the animal’s own rhythm, and ends with observations. There is no need to put an animal on camera or ask them to sit still.

Before the session

Complete the guardian intake with your animal’s name and your intention. Keep their usual routine as undisturbed as possible.

During the session

Your companion can sleep, move around, play, eat, drink, or leave the room. There is no need to restrain them or bring them back.

What if my animal moves away?

That is fine. Animals can choose where to settle or leave the space. We do not interpret every movement as a sign; let your companion keep their usual routine.

After the session

Allow your animal to return to their normal rhythm. Session observations are not veterinary findings or diagnoses. If you are concerned about your animal’s health, contact a veterinarian.
