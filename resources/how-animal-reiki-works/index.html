Animal Reiki guide

How does remote Animal Reiki work?

Your animal does not need to understand Reiki, sit still, or interact with a practitioner on a screen. A remote session lets them remain in their familiar environment and participate on their own terms.

Before the session

You’ll complete a short guardian intake so I know who the session is for and the intention you are bringing. Keep your animal’s normal routine as undisturbed as possible.

During the session

Your companion can sleep, walk away, eat, drink, play, or move between rooms. There is no need to restrain them or repeatedly bring them back.

After the session

Allow your animal to return to their ordinary rhythm. Session observations should never be interpreted as veterinary findings or diagnoses.

Veterinary care comes first: Animal Reiki is a complementary spiritual wellness practice and cannot diagnose or treat illness.
