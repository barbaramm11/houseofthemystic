What Is a Tarot Reading? A Grounded Guide

A grounded starting point for your curiosity.

A tarot reading uses a deck of illustrated cards as a starting point for conversation and reflection. In my intuitive readings, you can bring a question or choose a general reading. We look at the cards together, consider the symbols and patterns, and leave room for your own interpretation.

What can I ask?

A current decision, relationship dynamic, or repeating pattern can be a useful focus. Open questions such as “What am I overlooking?” often make more room for reflection than a request for a guaranteed yes or no. You can share up to two questions after booking.

What happens during the appointment?

We clarify your focus, explore the cards, and discuss what is useful to you. You can ask follow-up questions within the 45-minute session. Intuitive impressions may arise, but there is no required experience or particular outcome.

Can tarot predict the future?

I do not promise exact events, decisions, or contact with another person through a tarot reading. You keep agency over your choices and can combine the conversation with practical information from your life.
