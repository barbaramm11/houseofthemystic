What is Animal Reiki?

Animal Reiki is a complementary energy practice offered with respect for an animal’s autonomy, comfort, and natural pace. Your pet does not need to stay still, behave a certain way, or perform during the experience.

How remote Animal Reiki works

1. Tell me about your companion

Complete a short guardian intake with your animal’s name, species, temperament, and your intention.

2. Keep things natural

Your pet can remain in a familiar environment. There is no need to force them to lie down or stay in one place.

3. Receive

During the session your animal may rest, move, eat, play, or disengage naturally.

4. Integrate

Afterward, allow your companion to return to their normal rhythm and receive brief session observations.

Animal Reiki does not replace veterinary care. It is not veterinary medicine and should never delay diagnosis, treatment, medication, or emergency care from a licensed veterinarian.

Animal Reiki for cats

Cats often value choice, distance, and control over their environment. Remote work allows them to remain in their own space without restraint or forced interaction.

Animal Reiki for dogs

Dogs can participate from the comfort of home without traveling to an unfamiliar environment. They remain free to move and settle however they naturally choose.

Frequently asked questions

Does my pet need to stay still?

No. Animals may move, sleep, eat, drink, play, or leave the area naturally.

Can Animal Reiki treat illness?

No. It is a complementary spiritual wellness practice, not veterinary diagnosis or treatment.

Do you need to touch my animal?

No. These sessions are designed to be offered remotely.
