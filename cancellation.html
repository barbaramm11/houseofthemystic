Cancellation & Rescheduling Policy

Draft policy: edit the timing and rules to match how you want to operate before publishing.

Rescheduling

Sessions may be rescheduled with at least 24 hours’ notice, subject to availability.

Cancellations

Client cancellations and missed appointments are non-refundable. Appointments canceled with less than 24 hours’ notice may be forfeited.

Late Arrival

If a client arrives late, the session may still end at the originally scheduled time so the next appointment is not affected.

Provider Cancellation

If House of the Mystic must cancel or substantially reschedule a paid appointment, the client may choose a new available appointment time or receive a refund for the affected session.
