Evidential Mediumship

A gentle space to explore a possible connection with loved ones who have died, beginning with specific impressions and recognition.

45 minutes$95Online · English or Spanish

In an evidential mediumship reading, I share specific impressions as they arise and give you space to say what you recognize. I do not ask for a loved one’s name, photograph, or story beforehand. You are free to consider the experience within your own beliefs and boundaries.

The experience

I share impressions and specific details as they arise so you can consider whether you recognize a connection. A particular person, detail, or message cannot be guaranteed. The session honors your beliefs, grief, and boundaries.

What I’ll ask before we meet

Only your name, contact details, preferred language, and accessibility needs. Please do not send names, photographs, or stories of loved ones ahead of a reading.

Preparing for your session

If it feels meaningful to you, speak privately to your loved ones ahead of time and invite them to join us. This is an optional personal ritual; it cannot guarantee a connection.

All offerings are for spiritual reflection or complementary wellness. They do not replace medical, veterinary, mental health, legal, financial, or career professionals. Read session policies.

Common questions

Can I request a particular loved one?

You may hold someone in mind, but I cannot promise who—or whether anyone—will be recognized in a session.

Should I tell you their story before we meet?

Please do not send names, photos, or identifying stories. If it is meaningful to you, you can privately invite loved ones to join us before the appointment; it is an optional ritual, not a guarantee.
